How to request deletion

To delete your account and related cloud data, email us.

Email deletion request

Address: idoyun781@gmail.com

Include in your email

  • Subject: Dearly account deletion request
  • The email used on the account
  • If you write from another address, which Dearly account to delete

What we delete

  • Cloud profile (nickname, character, Google account link)
  • Display name on Together memberships
  • Push notification token

What may remain

  • Purchase records required by law, tax rules, or the store
  • Cheers you wrote in a Together room until that room is deleted. If you ask, we remove your name from them.
  • On-device archive, widget, and language settings are removed when you uninstall or clear app data

Timing

We process requests within a reasonable time after we confirm them. We will tell you if a record must be kept by law.

Subscriptions

Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Play Store.

宛先: idoyun781@gmail.com

  • 件名: Dearly account deletion request
